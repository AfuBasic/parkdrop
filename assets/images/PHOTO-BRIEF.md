# PHOTO BRIEF FOR PARKDROP MARKETING HOMEPAGE

**Note to owner:** You don't need professional equipment, just a recent smartphone. Shoot in good natural daylight. Avoid capturing unrelated brands, people's faces who haven't consented, or messy environments that look unprofessional.

**General Rules:**
- Get verbal consent from anyone whose face or distinguishing features will be visible.
- Strip EXIF and GPS data before uploading.
- No couriers, riders, motorcycles, vans, trucks, or warehouses.
- No posed smiles looking straight at the camera. Let it feel candid.
- Avoid poverty tropes. We want the park businesses to look busy, organised, and dignified.

---

### Shot 1: Hero Image (Required)
**Filename:** `hero-1200.jpg` (we will compress and generate WebP/AVIF versions)
**Orientation:** Landscape
**Subject:** An attendant at a pickup point inside a motor park.
**Action/Scene:** The attendant should be holding their phone, working. Parcels should be visible on shelves or stacked neatly in the background or foreground.
**Lighting:** Natural daylight.
**Composition:** Frame the subject in the **right two-thirds** of the image. Leave the left third relatively "quiet" (out of focus background, solid wall, or uncluttered space) so we can overlay the floating HTML status chips without obscuring faces or important details.
